Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Draft for project review

This page is a starting point, not legal advice. Before public launch, confirm it matches the data the live bot actually collects, stores, shares, and deletes.

1. Overview

Enlazo is a Discord bot designed for games, community interaction, progression features, and server utilities. This policy explains the general categories of information that may be processed when you interact with the bot.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on which features are enabled, Enlazo may process Discord user IDs and server IDs, command inputs, game results, XP and level progression, streaks, moderation actions requested by authorized users, and settings needed to operate features.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information through bot commands.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to run commands, maintain game sessions, calculate scores and progression, prevent abuse, apply server settings, and troubleshoot errors. The project should collect only what is reasonably needed for these purposes.

4. Storage and retention

Some bot features may store data in a database so scores and settings persist between restarts. Actual retention periods, backups, and deletion procedures must be documented according to the production deployment before launch.

5. Sharing

Enlazo does not need your Discord password. Data may be processed by Discord and the hosting or infrastructure providers used to operate the bot. Do not claim that no data is shared unless the production setup has been audited.

6. Your choices

You can stop interacting with Enlazo or ask the server's administrators to remove the bot. For data access or deletion requests, contact the project maintainers through the official project repository until a dedicated contact channel is published.

7. Children and safety

Do not use Enlazo to post personal or sensitive information about yourself or others. Server administrators are responsible for configuring their communities and using moderation tools appropriately.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as Enlazo changes. The date at the top should be updated whenever material changes are published.

9. Contact

For project-related privacy questions, use the official EnlazoBot repository ↗.
